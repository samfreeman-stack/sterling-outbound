Who we help

Commercial Gas Safety

Every commercial kitchen with gas appliances needs an annual safety inspection. We put you in front of the businesses who need it now.

A legal inspection cycle that never stops

Annual gas safety inspections aren't a courtesy — they're a legal requirement for any commercial premises with gas appliances, and landlords, operators, and insurers all rely on a valid, in-date certificate. Miss the renewal date and a kitchen can be forced to stop using gas equipment entirely.

Because the requirement is annual and premises-wide, the pool of businesses needing inspection renews every single year — restaurants, hotel kitchens, pubs, and care homes all cycle back through the same compliance calendar on repeat.

What we do for gas safety engineers

  • AI-personalized outreach to commercial kitchens approaching their annual inspection renewal window.
  • Automatic qualification, so only premises with a real, current inspection need reach your calendar.
  • Appointments booked directly — no chasing a callback that never comes.
