Who we help

Commercial gas safety

Commercial kitchens with gas appliances need regular safety inspection to stay certified and insured. We put you in front of the businesses who need it now.

An inspection cycle that renews every year

Regular gas safety inspection is standard practice across commercial kitchens, and for landlords and many operators it's a legal requirement under gas safety regulations. Insurers and letting agreements commonly rely on a valid, in-date certificate — and a lapsed one can mean a kitchen is forced to stop using gas equipment until it's resolved.

Because the cycle repeats annually, the pool of businesses needing inspection renews every year — restaurants, hotel kitchens, pubs, and care homes all cycle back through the same calendar on repeat.
