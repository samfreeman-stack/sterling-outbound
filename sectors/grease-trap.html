Who we help

Grease Trap Servicing

We help you become the obvious call before a blocked drain becomes their emergency.

Drainage compliance that catches up with you

Food premises are responsible for trade effluent compliance — and an unmaintained grease trap doesn't just risk a blocked drain, it risks fines, forced closure, and liability for damage to the wider sewer network. Water companies and local authorities take this seriously, and so do the insurers covering these premises.

The problem builds quietly until it becomes an emergency — which means most premises only think about servicing reactively, after a blockage. The businesses that get ahead of it with a maintenance schedule are the ones who never have that emergency in the first place, and that's a recurring, scheduled relationship rather than a one-off.

What we do for grease trap specialists

  • AI-personalized outreach to food premises with a genuine, ongoing grease trap maintenance need.
  • Automatic qualification, so only premises ready to commit to a servicing schedule reach your calendar.
  • Appointments booked directly — building a maintenance client base instead of one-off emergency callouts.
