Who we help

Grease Trap Servicing

We help you become the obvious call before a blocked drain becomes their emergency.

Drainage maintenance that catches up with you

Food premises are generally responsible for managing their trade effluent, and an unmaintained grease trap doesn't just risk a blocked drain — it risks fines, forced closure, and liability for damage to the wider sewer network. Water companies and local authorities take this seriously, and so do the insurers covering these premises.

The problem builds quietly until it becomes an emergency — which means most premises only think about servicing reactively, after a blockage. The businesses that get ahead of it with a maintenance schedule are the ones who never have that emergency in the first place, and that's a recurring, scheduled relationship rather than a one-off.

Who we target

Outreach goes to the person who owns kitchen operations day to day — typically the General Manager, Kitchen Manager, or Facilities Manager.
