Who we help

Kitchen Extraction & TR19 Compliance

Commercial kitchens are legally required to maintain extraction to TR19 standard. We connect you with the businesses that need it.

Recurring, legally mandated demand

TR19 (the industry standard for grease extraction cleaning) isn't optional guidance — it's tied directly to fire safety compliance and insurance validity for any commercial kitchen. Insurers routinely require proof of regular cleaning to a recognised standard, and failing to maintain it puts a premises' fire cover and food hygiene rating at risk.

That obligation doesn't go away after one clean. Most commercial kitchens need this work on a quarterly or biannual cycle depending on usage intensity — which means every restaurant, hotel kitchen, care home, and school canteen you win isn't a one-off job, it's a recurring relationship.

What we do for TR19 specialists

  • AI-personalized outreach to facilities managers at restaurants, hotels, care homes, and schools due — or overdue — for TR19-standard cleaning.
  • Qualification handled automatically — only genuine, capacity-matched interest reaches your calendar.
