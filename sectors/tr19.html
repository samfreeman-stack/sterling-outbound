Who we help

Kitchen extraction & TR19 compliance

TR19 is the industry-recognised standard for commercial kitchen extraction cleaning. We connect you with the businesses that need it.

Recurring, compliance-driven demand

TR19 cleaning is tied closely to fire safety and insurance requirements for commercial kitchens — insurers commonly ask for proof of regular cleaning to a recognised standard, and letting it lapse can put a premises' fire cover and food hygiene rating at risk.

That need doesn't go away after one clean. Most commercial kitchens work to a scheduled servicing cycle depending on usage intensity — which means every restaurant, hotel kitchen, care home and school canteen you win isn't a one-off job, it's a recurring relationship.

Who we target

Outreach is aimed at the people who actually control this decision — typically the Facilities Manager, Operations Manager, or Property Manager for a site or group of sites.
