How we qualify

We don't just book meetings. We protect your calendar.

Prospect→ Engaged→ Qualified→ Appointment

Before a conversation becomes an appointment, we weigh it against the criteria that actually matter to you: correct business type, relevant service, location, decision-maker, genuine interest, timeframe, commercial fit, and your current capacity.

We don't promise that every prospect will buy. We focus on controlling what we can: targeting, research, messaging, follow-up, qualification and reporting.
