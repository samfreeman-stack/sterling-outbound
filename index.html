Instead of asking you to take our word for it, here's what an actual campaign looks like from first contact to booked appointment.

Illustrative example — not a client result
Target
Multi-site London restaurant group (8 sites)
Decision maker
Operations Manager
Research
Group operates 8 restaurant sites across London. Extraction systems approaching their next scheduled cleaning cycle. No extraction contractor listed against the group's public supplier records.
Outreach

"Hi [Name] — noticed [Restaurant Group] has grown to 8 sites across London. Most groups this size are reviewing their extraction cleaning cover around now to stay ahead of the next inspection cycle site to site. Worth a short call to see if it's relevant?"

Response

"Hi, thanks for reaching out — timely, actually. Our current supplier hasn't been consistent across all sites. Can you tell me how you handle multi-site scheduling?"

Qualification Correct business type · Relevant service · In service area · Decision-maker confirmed · Timeframe: within 4 weeks
Result Appointment booked — Tuesday, 10:00, site walk-through with the Operations Manager. Straight into the contractor's calendar.

How we qualify

We don't just book meetings. We protect your calendar.

Prospect→ Engaged→ Qualified→ Appointment

Before a conversation becomes an appointment, we weigh it against the criteria that actually matter to you: correct business type, relevant service, location, decision-maker, genuine interest, timeframe, commercial fit, and your current capacity.

We don't promise that every prospect will buy. We focus on controlling what we can: targeting, research, messaging, follow-up, qualification and reporting.
