About

Why Sterling Outbound exists

I started Sterling Outbound because compliance-driven trades have one of the most reliable demand cycles in UK business, and almost none of them are treating it that way. TR19 extraction cleaning, gas safety inspections, grease trap servicing — these aren't optional extras. They're legal obligations that renew on a schedule. And yet most specialists in these trades are still finding work the same way they were a decade ago: word of mouth, the odd referral, waiting for the phone to ring.

That gap is the whole reason this exists. If the demand is predictable, the outreach that finds it should be too — run consistently, aimed precisely at the facilities managers and property owners who control these decisions, and handled end to end so it doesn't cost you a single hour of billable time.

We built this around AI specifically because generic outreach doesn't work on this audience. A facilities manager who gets a templated pitch ignores it. One who gets outreach that clearly understands their premises, their compliance need, and their timeline is far more likely to reply — and that's the difference an AI-personalized approach makes at scale, without needing a sales team behind it.

We're early. This is a new agency built on a specific belief about how compliance-driven trade demand should be pursued — not a large team with a long history behind it. What we do have is a system built specifically for these three sectors, and we'd rather be straightforward about where we are than pad this page with claims we can't back up yet.
