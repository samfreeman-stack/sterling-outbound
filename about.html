Who's Behind Sterling Outbound?

There's a real person accountable for this company. Here's who, and why it exists.

[FOUNDER PHOTO]
Sam Freeman
Founder, Sterling Outbound
Connect on LinkedIn

I started Sterling Outbound because compliance-driven trades have one of the most reliable demand cycles in UK business, and almost none of them are treating it that way. TR19 extraction cleaning, gas safety inspections, grease trap servicing — these renew on a schedule. And yet most specialists in these trades are still finding work the same way they were a decade ago: word of mouth, the odd referral, waiting for the phone to ring.

That gap is the whole reason this exists. If the demand is predictable, the outreach that finds it should be too — run consistently, aimed precisely at the facilities managers and property owners who control these decisions, and handled end to end so it doesn't cost you a single hour of billable time.

AI is part of how we do this — it helps us research prospects, personalise outreach and handle initial conversations at a scale that would be difficult to manage manually. It's not the pitch. The pitch is that specialist commercial contractors get more qualified conversations without having to build a sales function to get them.

Sterling Outbound is a focused specialist operation, built specifically around commercial outbound for TR19, gas safety and grease trap contractors — not a large team spread across a dozen industries. The business is deliberately staying focused while the system is refined with the right contractors, and we'd rather be straightforward about that than pad this page with claims we can't back up.

How we build trust

  • Specificity — we only work with three trades, so outreach speaks with real fluency about your market, not generic sales language.
  • Demonstration — you can see exactly what a campaign looks like before you commit to anything, not just a promise.
  • Transparency — we're clear about what's guaranteed (the process, the effort, the reporting) and what isn't (that any given prospect buys).
  • Accountability — there's a named person behind this business, reachable directly, not a faceless agency.
